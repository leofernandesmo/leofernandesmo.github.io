Research

My research explores software testing, automated reasoning, software security, and AI for trustworthy software systems — with a particular focus on Software Testing, Mutation Testing, Fuzzing, Automated Reasoning, Software Security, AI for Software Engineering, and Software Engineering for AI.

CPSoftware

I lead CPSoftware (Centro de Pesquisa em Software) , a research group officially registered with the Brazilian National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (CNPq). CPSoftware conducts research in software quality assurance, software security, artificial intelligence, data science, and software engineering tools and techniques.

Collaborations

Beyond academic research, I actively collaborate with industry and public-sector organizations through research, development, and innovation projects. Under CPSoftware, this has included partnerships with companies and government institutions to develop technological solutions and professional training programs in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, and 5G Networks — as well as a visiting research stint at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Porto (FEUP), from 2024 to 2025.
